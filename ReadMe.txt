How to Use:
	-SPACE or START-                 Join player at attract mode or start game at lobby mode
	-ESC or BACK-                    Switch from game mode to attract mode or shut down application in attract mode
	-Mouse or Right Stick-           Control player view
	-Left Mouse or Right Trigger-    Fire current possessed actors equipped weapon
	-A/D or Left Stick X Axis-       Move left or right, relative to player orientation
	-W/S or Right Stick Y Axis-      Move forward or back, relative to player orientation
	-SHIFT or Left Trigger-          Increase speed while held
	-SPACE or Gamepad-A-             Jump
	-1 or D pad-Up-                  Switch to weapon 1
	-2 or D pad-Down-                Switch to weapon 2
	-3 or D pad-Left-                Switch to weapon 3 (if you have)
	-4 or D pad-Right-               Switch to weapon 4 (if you have)
	-F-                              Toggle free camera mode (Single player only)
	-N-                              Possess next valid actor in map (single player only)
	-P-                              Pause the game
	-O-                              Single step frame
	-T-                              Slow down game while held
	-H-                              Speed up game while held
	-F1-                             Turn on debug draw, include FPS
